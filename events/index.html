THE ARCHIVES LEAVES THE BUILDING

The Archives is going out into the world.

Fraggle's Village and the wider Fraggle Archives are no longer confined to the shelves. From cat shows and harvest festivals to book markets and community events, the Archives is taking its stories, objects, and general institutional nonsense out into the field.
