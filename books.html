Fraggle's Village

A story of love, memory, and one very brave boy.

A small grey cat with a big personality, a fierce little hiss, and a life that mattered enormously to the person who found him.

His story became a book so that his life could continue reaching people long after he was gone.

No personal profit are made from the sale of this book. All profits go directly toward spay/neuter efforts for cats in need.
