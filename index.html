Founding Story

It Began With Fraggle

The Fraggle Archives is a place for stories worth keeping.

It began with Fraggle — a small grey cat whose life was far too short, but whose impact was anything but.

What started as a way to preserve his memory grew into books, artwork, rescue stories, strange little artifacts, and an entire archive dedicated to the lives that leave a mark on us.

Here you'll find the books of the Archives, the residents who call it home, the museum of objects and memories collected along the way, and the projects that continue Fraggle's legacy.
