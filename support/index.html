Support the Archives

Preservation is an act of care.

Every contribution helps the Fraggle Archives continue its work — preserving stories, building projects, supporting rescue efforts, and caring for the residents at the heart of it.

Help Keep the Archives Growing

Contributions help make future books, projects, memorials, and rescue efforts possible.

Support also helps provide veterinary care for the cats who are part of the Archives' story. Contributions may go toward veterinary bills, ongoing care, and other needs that help keep our residents safe, comfortable, and well cared for.

Every contribution, no matter the size, helps care for the lives behind the stories and keeps the work moving forward.
