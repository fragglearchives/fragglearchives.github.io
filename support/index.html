The Fraggle Archives exists because people choose to help preserve the stories, memories, and work that matter.

Help Preserve the Archives

If you would like to support the work of The Fraggle Archives, contributions help make future books, projects, memorials, and rescue efforts possible.

Support also helps provide veterinary care for the cats who are part of the Archives' story. Contributions may go toward veterinary bills, ongoing care, and other needs that help keep our residents safe, comfortable, and well cared for.

Every contribution, no matter the size, helps keep the Archives growing and helps care for the lives behind the stories.
