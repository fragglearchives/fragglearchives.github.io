THE MYTHOLOGY

Alternate Universe Archives Relics

Not every history belongs to this world.

The Alternate Universe Archives preserve artifacts and records from realities that exist alongside — or perhaps beyond — the Archives we know.

Their provenance is occasionally questionable. Their archival status is not.

The Hydration Poster

A document from an alternate reality in which the importance of properly regulated hydration infrastructure was apparently recognized at an institutional level.

The Dentistry Poster

A preserved artifact documenting dental policy from an alternate Archives reality.

The Exercise Poster

An archival document concerning physical activity in an alternate reality.

The Friendship Poster

An archival document celebrating friendship and companionship in an alternate Archives reality.

THE INTERDIMENSIONAL PRINCIPLE

Documentation does not require explanation.

The Archives record what they find. Reality may file an appeal.
