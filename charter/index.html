THE FRAGGLE ARCHIVES

The Charter

— ❖ —

A dedication to Seeing in a New Light, Recognition, and Meeting Them Where They’re At.

The Fraggle Archives exist to preserve not only the story of one remarkable little cat, but the way he taught people to see.

We believe that love begins with recognition.

That courage often looks ordinary.

That healing starts when we meet someone where they’re at, rather than where we wish they were.

We believe every hiss has a history.

Every scar belongs to a story.

Every frightened animal deserves the chance to become known instead of merely observed.

The transformation is not of the cat.

It is of the person looking.

We do not erase the journey to make it easier to look at. We help people learn to see farther.

Some legacies are measured by how many lives someone touched.

Others are measured by how many eyes they taught to see differently.

The First Day of the Archives was never about preserving the past.

It was about choosing the kind of eyes we wanted to carry into the future.
