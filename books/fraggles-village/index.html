The true story of Fraggle — a brave former stray, the people who came together around him, and the small grey cat whose life became the beginning of the Fraggle Archives.

THE STORY

One little cat. One enormous village.

Fraggle arrived carrying a difficult history, a fierce little hiss, and no reason to trust that the world had suddenly become safe.

Over the months that followed, people showed up for him — with veterinary care, patience, food, kindness, practical help, and the willingness to meet him where he was.

Fraggle's Village is the story of that life, the people who helped carry it, and what can happen when someone is truly seen.

THE VILLAGE CONTINUES

The story helps other cats.

Fraggle's Village was created to carry one cat's story forward — and to turn that story into practical help for cats still waiting for care of their own.

Personal and rescue fundraiser sales support cat rescue and veterinary-care efforts.

Different fundraising campaigns may support different rescue organizations or cats in care, allowing Fraggle's story to keep travelling where help is needed.
