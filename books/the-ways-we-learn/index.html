The Ways We Learn

Six cats. Six stories. A thousand ways to become yourself.

Coming September 2026

A collection of true stories about six very different cats, the lives they carried with them, and the lessons that emerged when they were given the chance to become fully themselves.

THE LESSON

Sometimes the teacher has whiskers.

The Ways We Learn is about recognition — the moment we stop asking an animal to fit the story we expected and begin paying attention to the one already standing in front of us.

Across six chapters, six cats become six teachers, each carrying a different history and offering a different way of understanding trust, belonging, commitment, courage, ritual, and the work of seeing clearly.

THE STORIES TRAVEL OUTWARD

The lessons keep moving.

The books of the Fraggle Archives are built to do more than preserve stories. Personal sales are used as fundraising opportunities for rescue organizations and cats in need.

The first personal-sale fundraising campaign for The Ways We Learn is planned to support Furry Tales Cat Rescue.

Furry Tales is part of the history carried by the book itself — an organization connected to the journeys of cats whose stories now continue through these pages.
